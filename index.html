Project at a glance

The portfolio follows the full design process from motivation and research to requirements, ideation, technical implementation, usability testing and final reflection.

Problem

Low exercise persistence

Many college students want to exercise but lose motivation because progress is invisible, guidance is unclear and routine check-ins feel unrewarding.

Solution

Gamified guidance system

The selected solution combines low-threshold check-ins, reward feedback, AI coaching and lightweight social interaction into one active campus experience.

Outcome

Human-centered iteration

User testing revealed key issues in visibility, motivation and interaction flow, leading to targeted visual and usability improvements.

Portfolio chapters
