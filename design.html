3.2 Design Alternative Scheme Comparison (3 Schemes & Final Selection Reason)

Scheme 1

Pure Functional Management-oriented System Mode

Operation Mode

Focus on student exercise data statistics, sports management and record functions, simple interface, no gamified design and social interaction modules, core functions are check-in recording and data query only.

Advantages

Simple development, stable system operation, clear management functions, low maintenance cost.

Disadvantages

No exercise fun, no user incentive mechanism, low active use willingness, unable to improve long-term exercise persistence, inconsistent with project gamified core positioning.

Scheme 2

Full Social Entertainment-oriented Fitness System Mode

Operation Mode

Focus on student social interaction, fitness challenge PK and community sharing, add a large number of entertainment gameplay and interactive activities, weaken professional fitness guidance and data statistics functions.

Advantages

High user playability, strong social interaction, high short-term user activity and attraction.

Disadvantages

Deviate from the core demand of improving fitness habits, excessive entertainment leads to ignoring exercise effect, insufficient professional guidance, not in line with campus fitness education goals.

Scheme 3 Final Selected

Gamified Incentive + Personalized Guidance + Lightweight Social Integration Mode

Operation Mode

Take low-threshold exercise check-in and gamified incentive as the core, match AI personalized professional fitness guidance and lightweight student social training partner modules, balance fun, practicality and professionalism, simplify operation and optimize user experience.

Selection Reason

This scheme perfectly fits the three core user requirements, forms a closed loop from exercise initiation, motivation maintenance to long-term retention, avoids the single defect of the first two schemes, not only ensures the fun and attractiveness of the system, but also meets the professional fitness guidance and campus fitness management needs, best matching the project design objectives and campus user actual scenario.
